Modules 2 through 4 covered the methods. This module puts them to work in a real operational setting: the factory. Manufacturing sits on a spectrum from bare-bones processing to full entrepreneurial risk-taking, and where a factory sits on that spectrum drives which method fits and how much margin it should earn. It also covers two manufacturing-specific tools: the Berry ratio and the idle capacity adjustment.
