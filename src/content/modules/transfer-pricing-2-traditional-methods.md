Three methods, one shared idea: find a price or margin that an independent party actually accepted somewhere in the real world, then use it as the yardstick. This module covers the CUP, Resale Price, and Cost Plus methods, the three traditional transaction methods that compare prices or gross margins directly, before Module 4 moves on to methods that compare net profit instead.
