Module 9 worked through a manufacturing case. This module switches to the distribution side, and to a different industry entirely, to show that the same five-step method applies regardless of what the group actually sells. It also introduces a genuinely tricky wrinkle: a loss-making subsidiary that might be a red flag, or might be sitting on a real, uncompensated intangible contribution of its own.
