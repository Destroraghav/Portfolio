The three traditional methods from Module 2 all need a reasonably close product or margin comparison. That breaks down fast once intangibles, unique functions, or genuinely integrated group operations are involved. This module covers the two methods built for exactly that situation: the Transactional Net Margin Method (TNMM) and the Profit Split Method.
