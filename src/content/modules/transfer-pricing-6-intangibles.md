Modules 1 through 5 kept circling back to one recurring obstacle: intangible assets are hard to price because they rarely have a real market twin. This module tackles that problem head-on, what counts as an intangible, who should actually be entitled to the profit it generates, and how you put a defensible number on something that's never been sold before.
