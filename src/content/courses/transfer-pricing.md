This course follows one fictional multinational, **Meridian Group**, through the core skills of transfer pricing: spotting when two companies are "associated," applying the arm's length principle, running a functional analysis, and pricing the deals a group does with itself. Every term is defined the first time it appears, every example uses original numbers, and every lesson links out to the primary OECD and EU sources.
