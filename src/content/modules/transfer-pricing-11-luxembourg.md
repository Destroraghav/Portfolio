Every module so far has built general, internationally-applicable knowledge. This one narrows the focus to Luxembourg specifically, its legal framework, its most distinctive area of transfer pricing practice (intra-group financing), and what compliance actually looks like in practice. If you only read one module closely before an interview for a Luxembourg-based role, make it this one.
