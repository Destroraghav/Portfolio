Not every controlled transaction involves a physical product. Groups constantly move money and support services between entities, loans, cash pooling arrangements, guarantees, shared back-office functions, and every one of those needs an arm's length price too. This module covers the three big categories: loans, cash pooling and guarantees, and intra-group services.
