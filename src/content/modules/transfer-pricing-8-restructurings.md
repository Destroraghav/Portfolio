Groups reorganise constantly: centralising R&D, converting a full-fledged distributor into a limited-risk one, moving a factory's decision-making to headquarters. Every one of those changes can shift real profit potential from one country to another, and both transfer pricing rules and domestic tax law have specific tools for making sure that shift doesn't happen tax-free.
